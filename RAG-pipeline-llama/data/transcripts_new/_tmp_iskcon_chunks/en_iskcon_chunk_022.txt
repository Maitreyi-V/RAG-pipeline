Karmajam buddhi yukta hi phalam tyaktva manishinah janma bandha vinirmuktaah padam gacchanti anamayam The wise engage in devotional service, take refuge in the Lord, and free themselves from the cycle of birth and death by renouncing the fruits of action in the material world. In this way, they can attain that state beyond all miseries. So when we renounce the action, The results of our actions, then we attain a stage beyond all miseries. Padam gacchanti anamayam.
